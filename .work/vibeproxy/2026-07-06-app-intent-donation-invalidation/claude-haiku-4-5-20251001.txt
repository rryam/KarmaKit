**Potential Issues:**

1. **CRITICAL - Privacy leak in gateFingerprint:** Line 3839 includes `subject.scopeID` in the fingerprint used for consent binding. While this enables scope-based invalidation, the `gateFingerprint` is computed from donation records which may be persisted or transmitted. If `scopeID` contains sensitive workspace/entity information, it could leak via fingerprint comparison or logs. The scope should be included in the donation identity hash, not exposed in plaintext within the gate fingerprint.

2. **Invalidation semantic gap:** The `invalidate()` method (line 3936-3943) matches records by `donationIdentifier OR scopeID`, but there's no guarantee that invalidation records are immutable or tamper-proof. An attacker could construct fake `CoreAgentAppIntentDonationInvalidationRequest` objects. However, this appears to be internal-only (not public API), so trust boundary may be acceptable.

3. **Missing invalidation of consumed consents:** When a donation is invalidated (line 3955), the corresponding consent receipt should also be invalidated. Currently, `CoreAgentAppleConsumedConsentReceipts` (line 2914) has no mechanism to clear consumed receipts on donation erasure, potentially allowing stale consent reuse if the donation record is re-created with the same ID after invalidation.

## Findings:

- **[HIGH]** Sources/CoreAgentApplePlatform/CoreAgentApplePlatform.swift:3839 — `gateFingerprint` includes plaintext `subject.scopeID`, which may leak scope/workspace identity information. Scope identifiers should be hashed or excluded from the gate fingerprint used for consent binding. Consider: `subject.scopeID.hashed()` or move scope validation to a separate, non-fingerprinted check.

- **[MEDIUM]** Sources/CoreAgentApplePlatform/CoreAgentApplePlatform.swift:2914-2927 — `CoreAgentAppleConsumedConsentReceipts` does not clear consumed receipts when corresponding donation records are invalidated. If a donation is erased and re-created with the same fingerprint, a stale consent from the invalidated donation could be replayed. Add a method to clear consumed receipts by donation ID, called from invalidation logic.

## Residual risks:

- Invalidation records are logged but never pruned; long-running services could accumulate unbounded memory.
- `CoreAgentAppIntentDonationRecord.init()` throws but does not document which errors are recoverable vs. fatal.
