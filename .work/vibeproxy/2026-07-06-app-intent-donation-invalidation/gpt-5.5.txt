Findings:
- [high] `Sources/CoreAgentApplePlatform/CoreAgentApplePlatform.swift:3788` `CoreAgentAppIntentDonationRecord` is `Codable` with synthesized decoding, so decoded/persisted records bypass the throwing initializer’s privacy and donation-policy validation. A decoded record can contain `subject.kind == .promptText` / `.toolArguments`, empty IDs, or an arbitrary plaintext/sensitive `donationIdentifier`, and `InMemoryCoreAgentAppIntentDonationStore.record(_:)` plus `CoreAgentAppleActionGate.appIntentDonationRecordDenial` do not revalidate those invariants. Fix by implementing `init(from:)` to validate the decoded subject and recompute/compare `donationIdentifier`, or add a public/internal `validatedForDonationRecord()` and require it in both the gate and store before accepting records.
- [medium] `Sources/CoreAgentApplePlatform/CoreAgentApplePlatform.swift:3920` invalidation is not durable against stale re-recording. `record(_:)` unconditionally re-adds a donation identifier/scope after `invalidate(_:)` removed it, so an old in-memory/persisted record can become active again after `.erased`, `.accessScopeChanged`, or `.userRevoked`. Fix by tracking invalidated donation identifiers and/or scopes as tombstones and rejecting/no-oping stale `record(_:)` calls unless a new authority boundary, policy version, descriptor revision, or explicit reauthorization path permits re-donation.

Residual risks:
